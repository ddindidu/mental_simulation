최종 진단 · F22

The patient has a persistent, circumscribed belief that dispatch and certain drivers are tracking and reporting on her in a targeted way. This belief is based on subjective interpretations (timing of radio comments, others' behavior) without direct confirmation, is held with strong conviction, and persists despite reassurance from her husband and lack of objective evidence. It is not a culturally sanctioned belief, is not better explained by generalized anxiety or a primary mood disorder, and is associated with functional decline at work and at home. The content is persecutory but systematized and limited to the work context, consistent with a delusional disorder (ICD-10 F22). Z73.3 (stress, not elsewhere classified) and Z56.6 (other physical and mental strain related to work) were considered but do not sufficiently account for the fixed, interpretive, persecutory belief and resulting impairment.
